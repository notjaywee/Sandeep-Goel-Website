## New copy (verbatim — 7 paragraphs, breaks exactly as shown)

I got off a plane in Canada in 2002 with ten years of experience as an electrical engineer in India, and nothing else. My first job here was on a warehouse floor. That's not a résumé line. That's how I know what it takes to build a life in Brampton from nothing, because I did it myself.

In 2004 I earned my trade licence and spent the next ten years at Litens Automotive as a maintenance technician. That same year, my wife Nisha and I opened our own business, Goel Travels, right here in Ward 4. I've called this ward home since 2005. It raised my two kids alongside me, and today my mother lives with us too.

I'm not a career politician. In 2016 I sat on Brampton's Blue Ribbon Committee and fought to bring a full university here, because our kids deserve that close to home. In 2022 I received the city's Inspirational Award, and I currently serve as a Brampton Business Ambassador. But none of that is why I'm running.

I'm running because Wards 3 and 4 deserve a councillor who actually answers the phone. Someone who has lived the same rising costs, the same commute, and the same worry about what's next for this community. Because I have.

Outside of all this, I'm just a guy who loves hockey and cricket, and who wants his kids, and yours, to grow up in a Brampton that's safer, more affordable, and still feels like home.

I'm asking for your trust and your vote, so we can build that together.

Thank you. I'll see you out there.

**Vote Sandeep**
Candidate for Regional Councillor, Wards 3 & 4, Brampton
