Who is Sandeep

A neighbour first, a candidate second

I landed in Canada in 2002 with ten years as an electrical engineer in India, and nothing else. My first job was on a warehouse floor. In 2004 I earned my trade licence and joined Litens Automotive. That same year my wife Nisha and I opened Goel Travels, right here in Ward 4. I've called this ward home since 2005. It raised my two kids, and my mother lives with us too.

I'm not a career politician. In 2016 I served on Brampton's Blue Ribbon Committee and fought for a full university here. In 2022 I received the city's Inspirational Award, and I served as a Brampton Business Ambassador. None of that is why I'm running.

I'm running because Wards 3 and 4 deserve a councillor who answers the phone, someone who's lived the same costs and worries about what's next.

I'm asking for your trust and your vote, so we can build that together.
