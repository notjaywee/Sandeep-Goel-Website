Who is Sandeep

A Neighbour First. A Candidate Second.

Canada became my home in 2002. I arrived with ten years of experience as an electrical engineer in India, but like many newcomers, I had to start again. My first job was on a warehouse floor, where I learned firsthand the value of hard work, perseverance, and opportunity.

In 2004, I earned my Canadian trade licence and began working with Litens Automotive. That same year, my wife, Nisha, and I opened our business in Ward 4, building a small business from the ground up and becoming part of the community we are proud to serve.

Since 2005, I have proudly called Ward 4 home. This is where my wife and I raised our two children, where we built our business, and where three generations of our family now live together. Brampton is more than the city where I live, it is the community that has shaped our family's story.

Read full story Show less

My commitment to Brampton has always gone beyond my home and business. In 2017, during Brampton's City Hall meetings, I strongly advocated for bringing a full university to our city, recognizing the importance of giving Brampton's young people greater access to higher education close to home. I have also contributed to promoting our city as a Brampton Business Ambassador, and in 2022, I was honored to receive the City of Brampton's Inspirational Award in recognition of my community involvement.

But awards and titles are not the reason I am running for Regional Councillor.

I am running because Wards 3 and 4 deserve accessible, accountable, and responsive representation.

Residents deserve a Regional Councillor who listens, answers the phone, understands the pressures families and businesses face, and works hard to deliver practical results. Having lived, worked, raised a family, and built a business in this community for more than two decades, I understand both how far Brampton has come and the challenges we must address for its future.

I am not a career politician. I am your neighbour, a local business owner, a father, a husband, and a longtime member of this community.

I am asking for your trust and your vote so that, together, we can build stronger neighbourhoods, create better opportunities, and make Wards 3 and 4 an even better place to call home.
